INTRODUCING iSeeGrass

Smart seagrass identification and assessment for marine research

iSeeGrass uses image-based processing and convolutional neural network classification to automate seagrass species identification from field images. The species library covers an estimated 60–72 species worldwide, while the identification model is trained and focused on species found in the Negros region. It supports researchers, students, and marine practitioners with accurate recognition, structured reporting, and faster data collection.

About the System

To address the challenges of manual seagrass identification, iSeeGrass proposes a hybrid web and mobile platform that brings automated species recognition into the field. The species information library covers an estimated 60–72 seagrass species worldwide — providing broad reference data for researchers and students. The CNN-based identification model, however, is trained and scoped specifically to species documented in the Negros region, keeping its classification focused and accurate for local field use.

With image recognition and CNN-powered classification, iSeeGrass delivers structured outputs that support data recording, analysis, and more effective assessment practices in marine research.
