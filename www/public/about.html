About the Project

What is iSeeGrass?

iSeeGrass is a web and mobile application developed to support seagrass species identification and ecological assessment using image recognition. It is designed for marine researchers, students, and field practitioners working with seagrass ecosystems.

Project Mission

Manual seagrass identification is time-consuming, requires expert knowledge, and is prone to inconsistency. iSeeGrass addresses this challenge by applying convolutional neural network (CNN) classification to automate species identification from field images.

The goal is to make seagrass identification faster, more consistent, and accessible — supporting better ecological assessment and research documentation in the field.

How the System Works

Image Capture or Upload

Users capture a seagrass image in the field or upload an existing photo through the web or mobile app.

CNN Classification with CREYNet

The image is processed by CREYNet — the system's Convolutional Recognition of Eelgrass Yards network — which classifies the seagrass species from the submitted image.

Species Information Retrieval

Once classified, the system returns detailed species information including common name, scientific name, taxonomy, habitat, and ecological notes.

Ecological Assessment Output

Identification results are used to generate structured assessment data, including species composition, abundance, and percentage cover for the sampled area.

Scope and Coverage

The iSeeGrass species information library covers an estimated 60–72 seagrass species recognized worldwide, serving as a broad reference resource for students and researchers.

The CNN identification model, however, is trained and scoped specifically for species documented in the Negros region of the Philippines — keeping its classification focused and accurate for local field conditions.

Who is it for?

Marine Researchers

Automate species identification during field surveys and generate structured data for analysis and reporting.

Students

Access a seagrass species library, practice identification, and learn about seagrass ecology through real images and structured species data.

Marine Practitioners

Support conservation monitoring, habitat surveys, and data collection with a tool built for practical field use.

Academic Context

iSeeGrass is developed as an academic capstone project. Its research objectives include evaluating the accuracy of CNN-based seagrass species classification and assessing the usability and effectiveness of the application among its intended users.

The system is not intended to replace expert ecological surveys or comprehensive field assessments. It is a tool to assist and support those processes.
