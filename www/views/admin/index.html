About iSeeGrass Platform

iSeeGrass is a hybrid web and mobile platform for seagrass species identification and ecological field assessment. It applies Convolutional Neural Network (CNN) image classification to automate species identification from field photographs, improving consistency and reducing reliance on manual expert review across surveys.

The platform supports structured data collection across multiple users and field teams. As an admin, you have full visibility into all assessments, user activity, and system data — and you are responsible for maintaining the quality and integrity of the seagrass catalog that powers the identification model.

Admin Responsibilities

  • Manage user accounts and roles.
  • Review all submitted assessments.
  • Maintain the seagrass species catalog.
  • Monitor identification activity and CNN usage.
  • Generate system reports and usage statistics.
ADMIN NOTE Admin actions are logged. Changes to user roles and catalog entries are permanent and affect all platform users.

Seagrass Species in Scope (Identification scope · Negros region · part of the broader ~60–72 species worldwide)

Enhalus acoroides

Tape Seagrass · Largest species in the world. Forms extensive intertidal meadows across Luzon, Visayas & Mindanao.

Thalassia hemprichii

Hemprich's Seagrass · Dominant species in Indo-Pacific mixed meadows; common across all Philippine regions.

Halophila ovalis

Dugong Grass · Pioneer coloniser with small oval leaf pairs. A primary food source for dugongs and green sea turtles.

Cymodocea rotundata

Rotund Cymodocea · Common on reef flats and shallow lagoons. Recognised by its rounded leaf tip.

Halodule uninervis

Spade Grass · Abundant pioneer species with a distinctive trident-shaped leaf tip. Key dugong food source.

Syringodium isoetifolium

Needle Seagrass · Unique cylindrical hollow leaves. Forms monospecific beds on sandy reef flats.
