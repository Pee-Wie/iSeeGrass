About the Identification Model

  • Classification is performed by a Convolutional Neural Network (CNN) trained on labeled seagrass images.
  • The model outputs a species prediction alongside a confidence score — lower scores suggest ambiguous images.
  • Only species present in the Negros region dataset are within the model's trained scope.
  • Results should be reviewed by the researcher; the system does not replace expert field assessment.
  • Assessment outputs include species composition, abundance, and percentage cover estimates.

Identifiable Species — Negros Region

These are the seagrass species the CNN model is trained to classify from field images. Part of the broader ~60–72 species worldwide catalogue.

Enhalus acoroides

Tape Seagrass · Largest species in the world. Forms extensive intertidal meadows.

Thalassia hemprichii

Hemprich's Seagrass · Dominant species in Indo-Pacific mixed meadows.

Halophila ovalis

Dugong Grass · Pioneer coloniser with small oval leaf pairs.

Cymodocea rotundata

Rotund Cymodocea · Common on reef flats and shallow lagoons.

Halodule uninervis

Spade Grass · Abundant pioneer with distinctive trident-shaped leaf tip.

Syringodium isoetifolium

Needle Seagrass · Unique cylindrical hollow leaves; monospecific beds.
